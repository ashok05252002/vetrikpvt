Features

Three apps. One platform.

Everything you need to run a delivery business — from the first tap to the doorstep.

Customer app

Browse shops by category, order in a few taps, pay online or COD and track delivery live.

Delivery partner app

Assigned runs, optimised stop order, navigation, proof of delivery and earnings.

Admin portal

Manage shops, customers, riders, orders, payouts and app content from one dashboard.

Multi-shop marketplace

Onboard any number of shops with their own categories, menus, cut-off times and status.

Scheduled delivery runs

Daily schedules and batched runs keep deliveries efficient and on time.

Payments & refunds

Online payments, transactions, shop settlements, returns and refunds — fully tracked.

How it works

From order to doorstep.

01

Customers order in seconds.

Nearby shops, home kitchens, groceries and medicines — all in one app, with clear cut-off times for same-day delivery.

  • Category & shop browsing
  • UPI, cards & cash on delivery
02

Shops stay in control.

Every shop is onboarded with its category, cut-off time and status. Admins see orders, active shops and exceptions at a glance.

  • Shop onboarding & master data
  • Exceptions flagged instantly
03

Riders deliver smarter.

Orders are grouped into delivery runs. Riders follow the route stop by stop while admins watch live tracking.

  • Daily schedules & runs
  • Live GPS tracking
04

Money, handled.

Transactions, shop settlements, returns and refunds are recorded automatically — and the CMS keeps app banners and offers fresh.

  • Transactions & settlements
  • App content (CMS)
