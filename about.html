Where it began

We started with one promise: build software so well that the client tells someone else about it. Every chapter below happened because someone kept that promise going.

The journey

Eight chapters, so far.

2024 Chapter 01

It started with one.

Vetrik began as a team of a single person: one laptop, a lot of late nights and a belief that small businesses deserve software as good as big companies get.

  • Founded in 2024
  • Team of one
Next Chapter 02

A full business system for 1000 Vibes.

For 1000 Vibes we built everything the business runs on: shop management, billing, inventory, customers, purchases and their website, all in one connected CRM.

  • End-to-end CRM
  • Shop management to website
2025 Chapter 03

A call from Ireland.

Then came our first international order: a complete food delivery platform for Ireland, with a customer app, a rider app and an admin portal to run it all. That platform became Home2Local.

  • First overseas client
  • Mobile apps + admin portal
Ireland Chapter 04

Cape Comorin Digital Limited.

To serve clients in Ireland properly, we registered Cape Comorin Digital Limited (CCD) there as our partner company, together with Rufus. It gives us a local home in Europe and a team on the ground.

  • Registered in Ireland
  • Partnership with Rufus
Then Chapter 05

Thenkadal Meenavan Seafoods.

We created Thenkadal Meenavan's brand identity and built their e-commerce store, taking a seafood business from the coast to customers' screens.

  • Branding
  • E-commerce website
Then Chapter 06

Tulasi Foundation, a landmark of development.

For Tulasi Foundation, a real estate builder, we created their complete branding and website. Now we're building two apps for them: visitor management for their apartments and their own CRM.

  • Full branding & website
  • Visitor management app, in progress
  • Custom CRM, in progress
Now Chapter 07

Chef2Comply: kitchens in Ireland run on our SaaS.

Chef2Comply, our food safety SaaS, helps restaurant kitchens in Ireland stay compliant, with digital temperature logs, checklists and sign-offs. Five restaurants came on board in the very first month.

  • 5 kitchens in month one
  • HACCP compliance, done daily
Today Chapter 08

Nine strong, and growing across India.

The team of one is now a team of nine passionate people. We're bringing our products home and expanding across India, and we still haven't spent anything on ads. Every client has come through someone who saw our work.

  • A team of 9
  • Expanding in India
  • Zero ads, all referrals
