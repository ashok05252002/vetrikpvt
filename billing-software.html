Features

GST invoices

How it works

A day at the counter, simplified.

01

Scan, bill, done.

Add items by barcode or search, apply discounts and take payment by cash, card or UPI in a few keystrokes.

  • Keyboard shortcuts for speed
  • Split & multiple payment modes
02

Stock that updates itself.

Every sale and purchase updates stock instantly. Get alerts before popular items run out.

  • Low-stock & expiry alerts
  • Purchase & supplier entries
03

Reports you'll actually read.

See today's sales, top items and profit at a glance. Export GST reports for your auditor in one click.

  • GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B summaries
  • Excel & PDF exports
04

Share bills instantly.

Print on thermal or A4, or send the invoice straight to your customer on WhatsApp, SMS or email.

  • Custom invoice designs
  • Paperless digital bills
