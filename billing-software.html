How it works

A day at the counter, simplified.

01

Scan, bill, done.

Add items by barcode or search, use retail or wholesale prices, and take cash, UPI or mixed payments in a few taps.

  • Retail and wholesale prices
  • Cash, UPI and mixed payments
02

Stock that counts itself.

Every sale and purchase updates stock instantly. FIFO costing on each purchase batch shows your exact profit, and low-stock alerts arrive before you run out.

  • FIFO costing per batch
  • Low-stock alerts
03

Reports you'll actually read.

Sales, margin and net profit at a glance. Export GST data for your auditor as a GSTR-1 ready Excel file.

  • GSTR-1 ready Excel export
  • Sales, margin and net profit
04

Cash locked, every day.

At closing, cash is counted, checked against the day's bills and locked. Any difference is flagged, and the audit log shows who did what.

  • Daily cash closing
  • Full audit trail
