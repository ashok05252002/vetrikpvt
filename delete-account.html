Account & data

Delete your account.

You can ask us to delete your Vetrik account and the personal data linked to it at any time. This page explains how, and what happens next.

How it works

  1. Send the request

    Use the form on this page or email support@vetrik.in from the email address on your account. Say which app you use.

  2. We confirm it's you

    We reply to confirm your identity and the account to delete.

  3. We delete your data

    Your account and personal data are deleted within 30 days, and we email you when it's done.

What we delete

  • Your account, login and profile details.
  • Business records in the account: products, stock, bills, customers, vendors, expenses, staff, attendance and payroll, and for Chef2Comply, logs, checklists, photos and sensor readings.
  • Support messages and enquiries linked to you.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Tax and invoice records (such as GST invoices for your subscription) for as long as the law requires, stored securely, then deleted.
  • Security logs for a limited time where needed to prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, so deleted data disappears from them as they cycle.
Business owners: deleting your business account deletes the records of all users in it. Export your reports to Excel first if you need them.
Staff users: to remove only your own access, ask your business owner to remove you, or contact us.

Want to delete only some of your data instead of the whole account? Email us and tell us what to remove. Read more in our privacy policy.

Request account deletion

Use the email or phone number registered on your account.

Your email app should open with the request. Press send and we'll reply to confirm.

Prefer email? Write to support@vetrik.in with the subject "Account deletion request".
