1. Who we are

Vetrik Solution Private Limited ("Vetrik", "we", "us") builds billing, shop management, food safety and custom software. We are based in Tamil Nadu, India.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, who we share it with and the rights you have. It is written to meet the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and the requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to:

  • Our website vetrik.in, including the contact and demo forms.
  • Our apps and software provided under the Vetrik name on the web, Android and iPhone, including our billing and shop management software and Chef2Comply.
  • Emails, calls and WhatsApp messages you exchange with us.

When we decide, and when your business decides

We are the controller (in India, the "Data Fiduciary") for your account details, website enquiries and the information we need to run and secure our service.

We are a processor for the business records our customers enter into our software, such as their own customers' names and phone numbers, bills, or staff attendance and payroll. The shop or restaurant that uses our software decides how that data is used and is responsible for it. If your details are stored in a business's account (for example, you are a customer or employee of a shop that uses Vetrik), please contact that business first. We will help them answer your request.

Apps we build for clients and publish under their brand have their own privacy policy from that client, and this policy does not cover them.

3. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, password (stored securely), business name, address and GSTIN.
  • Business records you enter: products, prices and stock; bills, payments and returns; customers, vendors and dealers; expenses and accounts; and, if you use the staff features, employee records, attendance, leave and payroll. For Chef2Comply: temperature readings, checklist entries, corrective actions, photos and sign-offs.
  • Enquiries and support: what you send through our forms, by email, phone or WhatsApp, such as your name, company, contact details and message.
  • Subscription details: your plan, invoices and payment status. Card or UPI payments are handled by our payment provider; we do not receive or store your full card details.

Information collected automatically

  • Activity and audit log: which user did what and when, and the IP address and device it came from. This powers the audit trail you see in the app and protects your account.
  • Device and diagnostic data: device model, operating system, app version, and crash and performance reports, so we can keep the app stable.
  • Sensor data (Chef2Comply): readings sent by temperature sensors installed in your kitchen.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, browsing history, health data or advertising ID, and we do not use precise GPS location.

6. Who we share information with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work for us under contract and only on our instructions, such as cloud hosting, data backup, email, SMS and WhatsApp messaging, payment processing and customer support tools.
  • Our partner company in Ireland, Cape Comorin Digital Limited, when it helps us serve customers in Europe.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users and the public.
  • A buyer or successor if our business is merged or sold, who must continue to protect your data under this policy.
  • Anyone else you ask us to, for example when you connect your online store.

7. Advertising, tracking and cookies

  • Our apps show no advertising and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Our website uses no advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's session storage only to remember a page transition while you move between pages; this is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles it.

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

8. International transfers

We are based in India and may store and process data in India and in other countries where our service providers operate. When personal data from the European Economic Area or the UK is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we use the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) or another lawful safeguard. Contact us for a copy of the safeguards that apply.

10. How we protect information

We use industry-standard safeguards: encryption in transit, secure password storage, role-based access so each user sees only what the owner allows, an audit log of actions, and automatic backups. Our staff can access customer data only when needed to provide support. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including for direct marketing.
  • Portability: receive your data in a common, machine-readable format, such as Excel.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier use.
  • Grievance redressal and nomination (India): raise a grievance with us and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot.

To use any of these rights, email support@vetrik.in from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We will reply within one month (sooner where the law requires), and there is no charge.

12. Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, from the app or on our account deletion page. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days. Records we must keep by law, such as tax invoices, are kept securely only for the required period and then deleted.

13. Children

Our software is built for businesses and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 in the EU or UK, or under 18 in India. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our products or the law change. We will change the effective date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app or by email before they take effect.
